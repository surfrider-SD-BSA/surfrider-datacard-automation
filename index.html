Beach Cleanup Data Cards

Drop in a scanned PDF and it finds every cell somebody wrote in, fills in what it can read, and builds the spreadsheet. Everything runs on this computer — the scan is never uploaded anywhere.

Most of the numbers are filled in and not shown to you. Where the tool is at least 75% sure of a reading it takes that as the answer and keeps the cell off the list — about 60% of a typical scan. Those values go into the spreadsheet marked as machine-read rather than typed, which is where they can be found afterwards; the handwriting reader is wrong about one time in six even at its most confident. What is left on the list is what it was less sure of: a picture, a box, and a tag saying which reader guessed.
