Beach Cleanup Data Cards

Drop in a scanned PDF — or a photo of a card — and it finds every cell somebody wrote in, shows you a picture of each one, and builds the spreadsheet from what you type. Everything runs on this computer — the scan is never uploaded anywhere.

Check every number it fills in. The tool now tries to read the handwriting, and it is wrong about one time in five. A filled box is a guess, not an answer — it is tagged, and the picture is right there beside it. Where it counts tally marks instead it is far more reliable, and those are tagged differently.
