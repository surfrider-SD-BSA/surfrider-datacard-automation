Beach Cleanup Data Cards

Drop in a scanned PDF and it finds every cell somebody wrote in, shows you a picture of each one, and builds the spreadsheet from what you type. Everything runs on this computer — the scan is never uploaded anywhere.

You still type the numbers. The tool does not read handwriting — it finds the writing and saves you the hunting. Tally marks are the exception: where it can count the strokes it fills the box in and marks it for you to check.
