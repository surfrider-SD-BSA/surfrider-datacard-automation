Beach Cleanup Data Cards

Drop in a scanned PDF and it finds every cell somebody wrote in, fills in what it can read, and builds the spreadsheet. Everything runs on this computer — the scan is never uploaded anywhere.

Most of the numbers are filled in and not shown to you. Where the tool is at least 45% sure of a reading it takes that as the answer and keeps the cell off the list — between two fifths and three fifths of a scan, on the three this was measured over. Those values go into the spreadsheet marked as machine-read rather than typed, which is where they can be found afterwards. Not all of them are right: checked against the chapter's own typed spreadsheets, between a fifth and a third of what this hides disagrees — some of that is the spreadsheets' own mistakes, and some a number the tool cut apart wrongly — and a clearly written digit on its own is misread about one time in thirty. What is left on the list is what it was least sure of: a picture, a box, and a tag saying which reader guessed.
