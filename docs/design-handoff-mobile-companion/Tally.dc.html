Concept · iOS companion

Tally

The data-card tool, on the phone that is already at the cleanup. Scan the cards, check the numbers against a picture of each one, send the chapter’s spreadsheet before you leave the car park.

What carried over from the repo

Nothing is guessed. The app decides whether a box was written in and shows you a picture of it — it never reads the handwriting, so it can never be confidently wrong. Pages that will not line up are refused out loud rather than cropped from. Typed work survives a closed app and is never restored unasked. The scan stays on the device.

What the phone adds

One cell at a time on a thumb-sized keypad, instead of hunting a 453-row list with a trackpad. Capture replaces the scanner and the Print-to-PDF trip that was throwing the resolution away.
