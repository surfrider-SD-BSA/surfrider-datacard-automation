Privacy Policy

Data Cards (Tally) for iOS · Effective 1 September 2026

This app collects no data about you. It makes no network requests of any kind. Everything it does happens on your device, offline, and nothing is sent anywhere.

That is the whole policy. The rest of this page explains what it means in practice, because a claim this broad deserves detail.

What the app does with your cleanup data

Data Cards reads scanned beach cleanup data cards and turns them into a spreadsheet. The scans you open, the readings the app produces, the corrections you type, and the drafts it saves between sessions are all stored in the app's own private storage on your iPhone.

They are not uploaded, backed up to us, analyzed, or shared. There is no server to send them to — the app has no account system, no login, and no remote component whatsoever. The reading and recognition run entirely on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically, the app does not collect, transmit, or store on our behalf:

  • Personal information of any kind
  • Usage data, analytics, crash telemetry, or diagnostics
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Contacts, photos, or any other library on your phone

There are no third-party analytics libraries, advertising SDKs, or tracking frameworks in the app. Nothing about your use of it is tracked, and no data is shared with any third party for any purpose.

Permissions

Files you choose. The app can open a PDF you pick yourself, or one you send to it from another app through the iOS share sheet. It only ever sees the file you deliberately hand it. It does not browse your files, and it takes a copy rather than opening the original in place, so it never has permission to modify your document.

A file shared into the app is placed in a private storage area shared only between Data Cards and its own share extension. The app moves it out and deletes the original the next time you open it, and anything left there for more than a week is discarded automatically.

Camera. This version of the app does not use the camera and will not ask for camera access. The app declares a camera usage description because photographing cards is a feature under development, disabled in this release.

Photo library. The app never requests access to your photos, and cannot read from or write to your photo library.

When you share a spreadsheet

When you export your finished spreadsheet, the app writes the file and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet. From there, you decide where it goes — Files, AirDrop, Mail, or another app you have installed.

Anything that happens after you choose a destination is governed by that service's own privacy policy, not this one. The app has no say in it and no knowledge of it.

Children

The app is a tool for recording beach cleanup survey data. It is not directed at children, and since it collects no data at all, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it does with data — for example, if the camera feature is released — this page will be updated before that version ships, and the effective date above will change.
